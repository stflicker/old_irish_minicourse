### Explicit carry-over after Day 028

The rest of Lesson 14 (especially 14.3-14.4 on io-stem adjectives and exercises) is **not skipped**. Begin there before Lesson 15. Lesson 9's varia, parts of Lesson 13, and other unselected exercises are held in a **set of extra exercises** to be used inside days rather than silently discarded.

### Quality control

- The terms and section numbers above follow Stifter's **2006 contents**, not a fabricated chapter order.
- A day's **target** is a teaching design, not a quotation of Stifter's paragraph.
- Page references are included only where directly checked; later lessons normally cite sections, which are edition-robust.
- Readings in the learner's book are referenced, not copied. If a source excerpt is unavailable, create a clearly labelled *constructed* exercise rather than misattribute it.
