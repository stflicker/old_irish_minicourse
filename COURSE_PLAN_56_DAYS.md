# Course plan: Days 001-056

Titles and subtitles only. Days 001-007 and 012 are written lessons (approved wording, kept verbatim). Days 008-011 and 013-056 are plans: the lesson files for Days 008-028 still use the older wording and will be rewritten in the new style (standard terms, all five cases in each form table, one number per session) when we reach them. Days 029-056 have no lesson files yet.

Stifter section numbers refer to the 2006 contents. Day types: N noun, S syntax/small words, V verb, H historical, T text reading/review.

## Review of Days 001-028 (what changed and why)

The first 28 days are feasible: each day has one target, nouns and verbs alternate, and history comes only after the forms are known. Small changes:

- **Day 021** repeated earlier material. It is now a short cross-language comparison of the feminine a-stems, parallel to Day 007.
- **Day 024** claimed too much about a debated topic. It is now "what is known and what is debated" about the two ending series.
- **Day 025** teaches the whole possessive table and the trap that *a* means 'his/her/its/their', with a different mutation each time.
- **Day 026** is a reading day. Stifter's poem is quoted only in short pieces; the rest is referenced, not copied.
- **Subtitles of Days 008-028** are rewritten in plain language. Terms like "revealing cell" are gone.
- **Days 012-014** (verb road map, then W1 present singular and plural) are as agreed in v0.4, still awaiting confirmation.

Days 029-056 follow Stifter's lessons 14.3-24 in order. Stifter's tests and exercises (22.5, 22.6, 26.1, and the exercise sections) are not separate days; they go into a practice pool used for review inside the days.
