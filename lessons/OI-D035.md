## Paradigm

Mutation of the next word: [L] lenition · [N] nasalization · [H] aspiration (h- before a vowel, gemination of a consonant) · [0] none. The letters show how the *next* word is mutated, not how the noun itself changes.

## One point

### The a-stem mutations with an e/i alternation

The mutations are exactly those of the a-stems (Days 15–17): nom [L], gen [H], prep [L], acc [N] in the sg; nom [H], gen [N], prep [0], acc [H] in the pl What is new is an alternation of *-e* and *-i* in the ending, which does not follow the io-stem pattern. The root vowel does not change. Pal (*guide*) or npal (*ungae*) is a property of the word.

**Quick answers**

- **Are there masc ia-stems?** No: ia-stems are exclusively fem.
- **What do ia-stems often express?** Abstract nouns made from adjectives: *slán* ‘whole’ → *slaine* ‘soundness’; *ainairt* ‘weak’ → *ainairte* ‘weakness’.
- **Where do the pl endings come from?** The nom, voc and acc pl were taken over from the i-stems (Day 37).
