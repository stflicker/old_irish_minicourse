## Paradigm

Mutation of the next word: [L] lenition · [N] nasalization · [H] aspiration (h- before a vowel, gemination of a consonant) · [0] none. The letters show how the *next* word is mutated, not how the noun itself changes.

## One point

### An unmarked singular, an ending only in the genitive

In the singular the nominative, prepositional, accusative and vocative end in the palatalized stem-final consonant; only the genitive adds an ending (*-o*, later *-a*) to the non-palatalized consonant. In the plural the endings *-i*, *-e*, *-ib* follow a consonant that is either always palatalized (*súil*) or always non-palatalized (*cnaim*, especially after *a*, *ó* and consonant clusters). Words in *-g* show a special alternation in the root vowel: *ai* before palatalized *g*, *e* before non-palatalized: *aig* ‘ice’, gen. *ego*; *liaig* ‘doctor’, gen. *lego*, nom. pl. *legai*.

**Quick answers**

- **Do all three genders have i-stems?** Yes: neuter in Day 44.
- **Which differences matter in practice?** Palatalization of the plural consonant, and the nominative effect.
- **Are there i-stem adjectives?** Yes, see Day 45.
