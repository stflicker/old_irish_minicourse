## One point

### A third large group of adjectives

Besides o-/a-adjectives and io-/ia-adjectives, i-stem adjectives are the third and last large adjective group. They differ a little from i-stem nouns: in the genitive singular they take the endings of the o-/a-adjectives (*maith* masculine, *maithe* feminine), and in the genitive plural either the noun ending *-e* or no ending. The palatalized stem-final consonant (*maith*) or non-palatalized one (*allaid*, where an ending is added) is a property of the word. Other adjectives of this type: *sainemail* ‘excellent’ (fem. gen. *sainemlae*), *sochraid* ‘beautiful’ (fem. gen. *sochraide*).

**Quick answers**

- **How many adjective types are there now?** Three: o-/a-, io-/ia- and i-stems (Day 52 shows how to decide).
- **Is there a feminine i-stem adjective ending in -i?** No; Stifter’s table gives the forms above.
- **What about the plural vocative?** Treated like the nominative plural in the table; the text layer is ambiguous.
