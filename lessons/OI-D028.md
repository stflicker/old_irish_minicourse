## Paradigm

Mutation of the next word: [L] lenition · [N] nasalization · [H] aspiration (h- before a vowel, gemination of a consonant) · [0] none. The letters show how the *next* word is mutated, not how the noun itself changes.

## One point

### Neut io-stems parallel masc io-stems as neut o-stems parallel masc o-stems

The neut nom and acc are identical, and they cause the same mutations as in *cenn* ([N] sg, [L] pl). The mnemonic is *cenn*: *cenn* (e), *cinn* (i), *ciunn* (u). Again the consonant before the ending is pal (*cride*) or npal (*cumachtae*) throughout the paradigm, and the root vowel does not change (§14.2).

**Quick answers**

- **Is there a long pl like *cenna*?** Not in the table: the pl of the neut io-stems has the same form as the sg
- **How do I tell cride [N] from cride [L]?** By the mutation of the next word, since the forms are spelled alike.
- **Which forms differ by number?** The prep (-iu / -ib) and the gen (-i / -e).

## Comparison (optional)

### Historical development

Neut io-stems stand to the masc io-stems as the neut o-stems to the masc (Day 4). The gen and prep forms continue the same endings as the masc.

**Summary.** Regular development; the mutations are those of *cenn*.
