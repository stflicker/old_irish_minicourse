## Paradigm

Mutation of the next word: [L] lenition · [N] nasalization · [H] aspiration (h- before a vowel, gemination of a consonant) · [0] none. The letters show how the *next* word is mutated, not how the adjective itself changes.

### *buide* ‘yellow’ · ia-stem adjective · fem

| Case | Sg form + effect | Pl form + effect |
|---|---|---|
| Nom | **buide** [L] | **buidi** [H] |
| Gen | **buide** [H] | **buide** [N] |

## One point

### Three adjective types now agree with all three genders

The same adjectives that follow the io-stem pattern with masc and neut nouns (Day 29) follow the ia-stem pattern with fem nouns. So an adjective in *-e* gives the full agreement set: masc/neut io-stem, fem ia-stem. The endings of the fem are those of the nouns of Day 35.

**Quick answers**

- **Do the mutations differ from the nouns?** No.
- **Is there a pl difference between masc and fem?** Yes: the nom pl masc ends in *-i* [L], the fem in *-i* [H].
- **How do I recognize the type of an adjective?** By its nom sg in *-e* (Day 52).
