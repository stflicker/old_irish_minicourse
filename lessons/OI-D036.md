## Paradigm

Mutation of the next word: [L] lenition · [N] nasalization · [H] aspiration (h- before a vowel, gemination of a consonant) · [0] none. The letters show how the *next* word is mutated, not how the adjective itself changes.

### *buide* ‘yellow’ · ia-stem adjective · feminine

| Case | Singular form + effect | Plural form + effect |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | **buide** [L] | **buidi** [H] |
| Genitive | **buide** [H] | **buide** [N] |
| Prepositional | **buidi** [L] | **buidib** [0] |
| Accusative | **buidi** [N] | **buidi** [H] |
| Vocative | **a buide** [L] | **a buidi** [H] |

## One point

### Three adjective types now agree with all three genders

The same adjectives that follow the io-stem pattern with masculine and neuter nouns (Day 29) follow the ia-stem pattern with feminine nouns. So an adjective in *-e* gives the full agreement set: masculine/neuter io-stem, feminine ia-stem. The endings of the feminine are those of the nouns of Day 35.

**Quick answers**

- **Do the mutations differ from the nouns?** No.
- **Is there a plural difference between masculine and feminine?** Yes: the nominative plural masculine ends in *-i* [L], the feminine in *-i* [H].
- **How do I recognize the type of an adjective?** By its nominative singular in *-e* (Day 52).
