## Paradigm

Mutation of the next word: [L] lenition · [N] nasalization · [H] aspiration (h- before a vowel, gemination of a consonant) · [0] none. The letters show how the *next* word is mutated, not how the noun itself changes.

### The article ‘the’ · feminine

| Case | Singular article + effect | Plural article + effect |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | **in** [L] | **na** [H] |
| Genitive | **na** [H] | **na** [N] |
| Prepositional | **-(s)in** [L] | **-(s)naib** [0] |
| Accusative | **in** [N] | **na** [H] |
| Vocative | **a** [L] (particle; no article) | **a** [L] (particle; no article) |

Examples with *tuath*: *in tuath* ‘the tribe’, *na tuaithe* ‘of the tribe’, *don tuaith* ‘to the tribe’, *in tuaith* ‘the tribe’ (object); plural *na tuatha*, *na tuath*, *donaib tuathaib*.

### Variants before certain sounds (singular)

| The next word begins with | Nominative | Prepositional |
|---|---|---|
| b, d, g, p, t, c, m | **in** | **-(s)in** |
| a vowel, l, r, n, f | **ind** | **-(s)ind** |
| s | **int** | **-(s)int** |

Examples: *ind ires* ‘the belief’, *ind lám* ‘the hand’, *ind rún* ‘the secret’, *ind náire* ‘the shamefulness’, *ind llesc* ‘the rod’, *int súil* ‘the eye’; prepositional *dond náire*, *cosind llesc*, *isint súil*.

## One point

### The feminine article has the effects of the a-stem

The mutations of the feminine article are those of the a-stem noun (Stifter §11.1, note 1): nominative singular [L], genitive singular [H], prepositional [L], accusative [N]. So the articles *in* [0] (masculine) and *in* [L] (feminine) are spelled alike, and the mutation of the next word, or the noun itself, tells the gender.

**Quick answers**

- **Why *na* and not *inna*?** In the oldest texts *inna* has two syllables; the common form is *na* (§11.2).
- **Is there an article in the vocative?** No.
- **How is the article used with prepositions?** Always fused; see Day 19.

## Comparison (optional)

### Historical development: the feminine article

| Case | Proto-Celtic (Stifter) | Old Irish |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative singular | \*(s)indā | **in** [L] |
| Genitive singular | \*(s)indās | **na** [H] |
| Accusative singular | \*(s)indam | **in** [N] |
| Nominative plural | \*(s)indās | **na** [H] |

The feminine article continues the Proto-Celtic demonstrative \*(s)indā- with a-stem endings, so its effects are those of the a-stem noun (compare Day 8 for the masculine).
