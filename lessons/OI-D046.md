## One point

### Four prepositions, two case-sets

*Ar*, *fo*, *for* and *in* are the four prepositions that can take both cases: the prepositional expresses position, the accusative direction. In the conjugated forms the 1st and 2nd persons are the same for both, while the 3rd persons always differ: *indib* ‘in them’, *intiu* ‘into them’. A few forms are not attested (the 3rd feminine prepositional of *ar* and *fo*). Remember that all the conjugated prepositions have to be learned individually (Day 38).

**Quick answers**

- **Do *la*, *fri* and *tre* take one case?** Yes: the accusative, so only one set of 3rd person forms exists.
- **What about 3rd pl. -u and -ib?** The accusative takes -u (*friu*, *forru*), the prepositional -ib (*indib*, *foraib*).
- **Which forms were used in the oldest texts?** Stifter gives variants; the shorter forms are younger.
