## One point

### Same endings, different sources

S1 and S2 are old thematic verbs, differing in the suffix (\*-e/o- versus \*-i̯e/o-): the S1 3sg continues \*-eti, the S2 \*-ieti. The pre-Celtic input forms are fairly certain. The intermediate stages between them and Old Irish are Stifter’s own reconstructions and contested; for S3 he reconstructs no further back than Insular Celtic because the PIE input formations are very diverse. Do not memorize the intermediate forms; the point of the day is the limit of the evidence.

**Quick answers**

- **Where do the conj endings come from?** From the same forms with a different development (Day 24).
- **What is ‘Insular Celtic’?** The stage common to the ancestors of Irish and the British languages, such as Welsh.
- **Should I memorize the reconstructions?** No.
