## Paradigm

Mutation of the next word: [L] lenition · [N] nasalization · [H] aspiration (h- before a vowel, gemination of a consonant) · [0] none. The letters show how the *next* word is mutated, not how the noun itself changes.

### *tuath* ‘people, tribe, petty kingdom’ · fem a-stem · sg

| Case | Sg form + effect |
|---|---|
| Nom | **tuath** [L] |
| Gen | **tuaithe** [H] |
| Prep | **tuaith** [L] |
| Acc | **tuaith** [N] |
| Voc | **a thuath** [L] |

The fem a-stem is more regular than the o-stem declension: two model words are enough (*tuath* here, *cell* on Day 16). The root-final consonant is pal in the gen, prep and acc sg

## One point

### Fem nouns show the case in the form itself

In the masc o-stem nom and acc of *ech* look alike, and only the next word tells them apart. In *tuath* they differ in spelling (*tuath* / *tuaith*) and in mutation ([L] / [N]). The prep *tuaith* [L] and the acc *tuaith* [N] are spelled alike, so again the mutation of the next word decides.

**Quick answers**

- **Why is the nom lenited-effect [L]?** Because the old final long vowel *-ā was lost, and a lost vowel leaves lenition (compare Day 1).
- **Is the gen always pal?** No: words with a consonant cluster at the end do not palatalize (*delb*, gen *delbae*); see Day 16.
- **Does *tuath* have a long form of the pl?** The pl is taught on Day 16.

## Comparison (optional)

### Historical development: *tuath*

| Case | PIE | Proto-Celtic | Old Irish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nom | \*teuteh₂ | \*toutā | **tuath** [L] |
| Gen | \*teuteh₂s | \*toutās | **tuaithe** [H] |
| Prep | \*teuteh₂ei, -eh₂i | \*toutai « | **tuaith** [L] |
| Acc | \*teuteh₂m | \*toutam | **tuaith** [N] |
| Voc | \*teute(h₂) | \*toutā | **a thuath** [L] |

Forms from Stifter p. 59. « = replaced by analogy.

1. **Nom and voc.** The final vowel \*-ā was lost and left lenition [L].
2. **Gen.** \*-ās > \*-ah gave [H]; the palatalization of the consonant and the ending -e come from the gen form.
3. **Acc.** \*-am > \*-an: a nasal, so [N].
4. **Prep («).** The form \*-ai replaced the regular result of PIE \*-eh₂ei.

**Summary.** Regular sound change: 1–3. Analogy («): the prep.

*Sources:* Stifter (2006) §10.1, p. 59.
