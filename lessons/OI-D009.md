## Paradigm

Mutation of the next word: [L] lenition · [N] nasalization · [H] aspiration (h- before a vowel, gemination of a consonant) · [0] none. The letters show how the *next* word is mutated, not how the article itself changes.

### The article ‘the’ · masculine · plural (before b, d, g, p, t, c, m)

| Case | Plural article + effect | Example phrase |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | **in** [L] | **in chaitt** ‘the cats’ |
| Genitive | **inna** [N] | **inna catt** ‘of the cats’ |
| Prepositional | **-(s)naib** [0] | **donaib cattaib** ‘to the cats’ |
| Accusative | **inna** [H] | **inna chattu** ‘the cats’ (object) |
| Vocative | no article | **a chattu** ‘O cats’ |

### The variants of the article (singular and plural)

| The next word begins with | Nominative singular | Genitive singular and nominative plural | Prepositional singular |
|---|---|---|---|
| b, d, g, p, t, c, m | **in** | **in** | **-(s)in** |
| a vowel | **int** | **ind** | **-(s)ind** |
| l, r, n, f | **in** | **ind** | **-(s)ind** |
| s | **in** | **int** (s > h) | **-(s)int** |

Examples (Stifter §7.2): *int en* ‘the bird’, *ind euin* ‘of the bird, the birds’, *ind rein* ‘of the sea’, *int sacairt* ‘of the priest, the priests’, *dond eun* ‘to the bird’, *dont sacart* ‘to the priest’.

## One point

### The plural article is again the o-stem pattern

The mutations of the plural article are those of the o-stem plural: the nominative plural lenites [L], the genitive plural nasalizes [N], the prepositional plural has no effect [0], the accusative plural aspirates [H]. The variants *int / ind* only depend on the first sound of the next word.

**Quick answers**

- **Why is *inna* sometimes written *na*?** In the oldest texts *inna* has two syllables; the most common form is *na* (§7.1 note 5).
- **Why *ind* before *f* and *l*?** Stifter gives the rule only as a list of initial sounds (vowel, l, r, n, f); he does not explain it further.
- **Is the vocative plural with an article?** No: the article cannot be used in the vocative.

## Comparison (optional)

### Historical development: the plural article

| Case | Proto-Celtic (Stifter) | Old Irish |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | \*(s)indi | **in** [L] |
| Prepositional | \*(s)indobis | **-(s)naib** [0] |

1. As with the noun, the prepositional plural ending is \*-obis (Day 2).
2. Stifter’s other plural forms are garbled in the text layer and are not given here.

**Summary.** The forms are the regular ones for an o-stem; the details of the variants are Stifter’s descriptive rules. Unresolved: why only these initial sounds take *ind*.
