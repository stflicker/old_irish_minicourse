# Verification notes (for maintainers, not shown to learners)

Lesson pages must not mention how the material was read or checked. Caveats about sources and uncertain forms are kept here instead.

## Source text
Stifter (2006) was available only as an OCR text file, with no page images. Superscripts, macrons, accents and some reconstructed forms are damaged in that text. Only forms legible there were used; reconstructions that could not be read were omitted. Check the items below against the printed book when it is available.
