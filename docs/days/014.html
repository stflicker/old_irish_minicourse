02 / FOCUS

One point

Here is a classic trap: caraid and ní·caraid have the same letters at the end, but different grammatical persons. The former is 3rd singular absolute; the latter is 2nd plural conjunct. Parsing starts with the left edge of the verb phrase.

A second useful reminder: the 2nd plural absolute ending has an assimilated spelling in glantae (compare regular carthae). The important thing is that both are 2nd plural absolute.

Two quick steps: (1) identify independent or dependent environment; (2) then assign person and number.

Sources: Stifter (2006), §8.4 and exercises in §§8.5, 9.2; all example glosses here are pedagogical.
